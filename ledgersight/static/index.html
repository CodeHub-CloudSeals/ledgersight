Where files can come from
Available now
  • CSV, TSV and text exports
  • Excel workbooks (.xlsx)
  • Bank downloads: OFX, QFX, QBO (QuickBooks Web Connect)
  • Invoice and receipt PDFs (text)
  • Customer & vendor list
  • Several files in one go
  • Saved emails (.eml) with attachments
  • Google Drive files and Sheets (once connected)
Planned for the pilot (read-only)
  • QuickBooks Online or Xero, whichever you use
  • OneDrive / Dropbox folder
  • Live receipts inbox (clients forward to an address)
  • Photos and scanned receipts (OCR)
Not accepted, by design
  • Macro workbooks (.xlsm) and old .xls
  • ZIP archives
  • PowerPoint and Word files: they don't hold transaction data
  • Tax returns, SSNs, bank logins
